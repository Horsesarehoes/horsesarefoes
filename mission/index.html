The Hoofprint Files

A collection of rural reports, archival oddities, trail-camera anomalies, and unexplained patterns involving horses, restricted landscapes, and the long-running question of Bigfoot.

What We Stand For

Absurdity First

If an event can be explained by normal barn behavior, we prefer the version involving enchanted carrots and suspicious hoofprints.

We keep the joke obvious. Horses Are Foes is parody, not a real investigative source.

Despite the name, we do not endorse annoying, bothering, or harassing real horses. They have enough going on.

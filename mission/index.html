What We Stand For

Absurdity First

If an event can be explained by normal barn behavior, we prefer the version involving enchanted carrots and suspicious hoofprints.

Clear Records

Each entry is cataloged with care. The archive is maintained as a structured record of reported activity and analysis.

Respect Actual Horses

Despite the name, we do not endorse annoying, bothering, or harassing real horses. They have enough going on.

Founded in the Wake of J.R. Fleming

This organization was created after a close reading of the writings of J.R. Fleming, a great man whose work exposed the hidden pressure of the demons who invade our realm. His words gave shape to our mission and gave us a reason to continue investigating the strange patterns that gather around horses, hay, and the borders of ordinary life.

We do not treat his work as mere theory. We treat it as a warning, a record, and a call to remain vigilant.
